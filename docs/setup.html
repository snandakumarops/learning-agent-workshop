Setup

🕓 7 minutes

Complete these steps before the workshop session so you're ready to go when we start.

Sign up for your workshop org

1 Open the sign-up URL

Navigate to orgfarm.salesforce.com/signup

2 Fill in the sign-up form

Enter the event code provided by your presenter, your email address, first name, last name, company, role, and country/region. Then click Submit.

You will receive the org credentials on the email you provide.

3 Sign-up successful

You should now see a signup successful message with a link to open the org directly. Alternatively, you can also check your email for the sign-up link.

These orgs will be available to use for 3 days following the event.

Save your credentials

Write down your username and password — you'll need them to log into your sandbox during the workshop.

Verify your org

4 Log into your org

Use the link from the success page or your email to log in. You can also go to login.salesforce.com and use the credentials from your sign-up email. You should land in your Salesforce org.

5 Confirm you see the app

Use the App Launcher (the grid icon in the top left) to search for and open Learning Management. You should see the Connected Executive Education dashboard with a Course Enrollments table.

Log into your sandbox

6 Navigate to Setup

In your org, click the gear icon in the top right and select Setup. In the Quick Find box on the left, type sandbox and click Sandboxes under Environments.

You will see two sandboxes listed. Use the fullcopy sandbox — click Log In next to it.

7 Log into the sandbox

Click the Log In link next to your sandbox. The password is orgfarm1234. If you're prompted to register your phone number, you can skip this step.

If you need to log into the sandbox later, go to test.salesforce.com. The username is your production username with .fullcopy appended (e.g. user@example.com.fullcopy) and the password is orgfarm1234.

Enable Change Management

8 Turn on Org-to-Org Metadata Deployment

Inside your sandbox, go to Setup → search for change in Quick Find → click Change Management under Development. Toggle on Org-to-Org Metadata Deployment (Beta).

This enables metadata deployment between your sandbox and production org, which we'll use later in the workshop.

Activate MCP Servers

MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers connect Agentforce Vibes to your org's capabilities. These platform MCP servers let the agent explore your org's objects, fields, and relationships. You need to activate two servers for this workshop.

9 Navigate to MCP Servers

In your sandbox, go to Setup → search for mcp ser in Quick Find → click MCP Servers under Integrations > API Catalog. You'll see a list of available servers, all with status Inactive.

10 Activate salesforce-api-context

Click salesforce-api-context from the list, then click the Activate button. This server provides Agentforce Vibes with Salesforce API context so it can generate code that works with your org's specific configuration.

11 Activate metadata-experts

Go back to the MCP Servers list and click metadata-experts, then click Activate. This server allows Agentforce Vibes to discover and inspect your org's metadata — objects, fields, relationships, and configurations.

What happens next?

We'll begin where all safe development starts — the sandbox. This is your isolated environment where you can experiment and iterate without risk to production. From here, you can open the Agentforce Vibes IDE right inside the sandbox, build your feature, and push from sandbox to prod.

Having trouble?

If you can't log in or don't see the Learning Management app, raise your hand at the start of the workshop and a facilitator will help you get set up.

Once you can see the dashboard in your sandbox, you're all set for the workshop.
