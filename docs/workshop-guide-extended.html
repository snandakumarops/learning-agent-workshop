Agentforce Vibes Workshop

🕓 ~2 hours

The Connected Executive Education team has two requests on the table today. You'll work through both — a simple metadata change and a full feature build — using the same governed, sandbox-first workflow for each.

Setup

1 Sign up for your workshop org

Navigate to the sign-up URL provided by your facilitator. Enter the event code and your email address — you'll receive org credentials by email. Click the link to open the org directly.

Save your credentials

Write down your username and password — you'll need them throughout the workshop.

2 Confirm the app is installed

In your production org, click the App Launcher (waffle icon) and search for Learning Management. You should see the Connected Executive Education dashboard with a Course Enrollments table. If you don't see it, raise your hand and a facilitator will help.

3 Log into the fullcopy sandbox

In your production org, go to Setup → Sandboxes. You will see two sandboxes listed. Use the fullcopy sandbox — click Log In next to it.

Inside the fullcopy sandbox, go to Setup → search for change in Quick Find → Change Management under Development. Toggle on Org-to-Org Metadata Deployment (Beta).

This enables metadata deployment between your sandbox and production.

Part 1 — Simple Metadata Change via DX Inspector  ~35 min

The ask

"We need a Deferred status on enrollments. Students are deferring to the next cohort — right now we're marking them Withdrawn, which is wrong. Can we get this into production today?"

This is the most common type of Salesforce request: a small, well-scoped metadata change that needs to go through the proper pipeline. We'll make the change in the fullcopy sandbox, verify it, then use DX Inspector to promote it to production.

1. See the Gap 3 min

5 Open an Enrollment record

From the fullcopy sandbox, open the App Launcher and search for Connected Executive Education. Open any Enrollment record and click Edit. Look at the Status dropdown — you'll see: Not Started, In Progress, On Hold, Withdrawn, Completed.

No "Deferred". That's the ask.

Why not "On Hold"?

On Hold is admin-controlled (e.g., payment issue). Deferred is student-initiated — they've chosen to move to a future cohort. The distinction matters for reporting and automations.

2. Add the Picklist Value 7 min

6 Open the Status field in Setup

In the fullcopy sandbox, click the gear icon → Setup. In Quick Find, type object manager and click Object Manager. Search for and open Enrollment.

Click Fields & Relationships, then click the Status field.

7 Add the Deferred value

Scroll to the Values section and click New. Enter Deferred as the label and click Save.

The value is now live in the sandbox. Open any Enrollment record and confirm Deferred appears in the Status dropdown.

3. Verify the Change 2 min

8 Check it on a record

Open any Enrollment record, click Edit, and confirm Deferred is selectable in the Status field. That's all the verification needed for a picklist value — it's live in the sandbox the moment you saved it.

Checkpoint

Deferred is visible and selectable. The sandbox has the change — now we need to get it to production safely.

5. Promote to Production with DX Inspector 10 min

8 Open DX Inspector

  1. Navigate to DX Inspector (your facilitator will provide the URL, or use the Chrome extension)
  2. Connect your source environment: the fullcopy sandbox
  3. Connect your target environment: production

9 Review the diff

DX Inspector shows exactly what differs between the two environments:

ComponentTypeStatus
Enrollment__c.Status__cCustomField (Picklist)Modified — new value: Deferred

Click the component to see the diff — the new <value> block highlighted in green. Confirm this is the only change.

10 Deploy to production

  1. Select the Enrollment__c.Status__c component
  2. Click Deploy to Target
  3. DX Inspector runs validation — confirm and proceed
  4. Once complete, open an Enrollment record in production and confirm Deferred is available in the Status dropdown
Part 1 complete

A business request went from conversation to production in under 35 minutes — through a sandbox, reviewed, and promoted with confidence. Now let's tackle the bigger ask.

Part 2 — Build a Feature with Agentforce Vibes  ~65 min

The second ask

"Program directors want a Top Learners section on the dashboard — top 5 students by completed enrollments, with their satisfaction ratings. Something that makes high performers visible."

This is a feature request, not a field change. It needs new Apex logic, LWC updates, and testing. This is where Agentforce Vibes earns its place: conversational scaffolding, live preview, iterative deployment — all inside the same sandbox-first workflow we've already used in Part 1.

6. Open Agentforce Vibes 5 min

Part 2 runs inside Agentforce Vibes IDE. Before we start building, get it running and activate the tools it needs.

19 Activate MCP Servers

In the fullcopy sandbox, go to Setup → search for mcp ser in Quick Find → MCP Servers under Integrations > API Catalog. Activate both:

  • salesforce-api-context — gives Agentforce Salesforce API context
  • metadata-experts — lets Agentforce discover org metadata (objects, fields, relationships)

20 Open Agentforce Vibes

From the gear icon → Agentforce Vibes. Once loaded, it's authenticated to the fullcopy sandbox. Select I agree to the terms and click Enable & Start Building.

Agentforce is your conversational coding partner. When you chat with it, you're not just getting suggestions — you're working with an agent that understands your project context and can execute entire development workflows through natural language.

The way it does this is through Model Context Protocol (MCP). Think of MCP like the USB standard for AI agents: just as USB gives your computer a consistent way to connect to any device, MCP gives Agentforce a consistent way to connect to different development tools.

Agentforce always works in two phases:

  • Plan — explains what it intends to do and why, without changing any files.
  • Act — executes those steps under guardrails, asking for confirmation at each stage.

11 Explore the MCP Server

Click "Manage MCP Servers". The Salesforce DX MCP Server is already added by default. Click the gear icon to explore its tools — scroll through the list to see what's available, then click Done.

12 Explore Agentforce Rules

Click on Manage Agentforce Rules & Workflows from the bottom pane. Inspect the rules — these are persistent guardrails that keep Agentforce aligned with your team's standards across every session.

7. Explore with Metadata Explorer 5 min

Before building, orient yourself in the org schema — the same way you would on a real project.

13 Browse the objects

Open the Metadata Explorer panel and navigate the three custom objects: Student__c, Course__c, Enrollment__c. Answer these questions:

  • How are the objects related? Which is the junction object?
  • What fields on Enrollment__c track completion and satisfaction?
  • What's the API name for the attendance percentage field? (You'll use it in the iteration step.)

14 Run a live query

Click on Enrollment__c and use the Query feature to run:

SELECT Student__c, Status__c, Satisfaction_Rating__c, Attendance_Percentage__c
FROM Enrollment__c
WHERE Status__c = 'Completed'
LIMIT 10

Get a feel for the shape of the data you're building against.

8. Scaffold Preview 7 min

Look at the Learning Management Dashboard in your browser — there's empty space above the enrollment table. That's where the Top Learners widget will go.

15 Enter Plan mode

From the bottom right of Agentforce, click Plan, then enter this prompt:

I want to add a "Top Learners" section to the existing learningManagementDashboard LWC.
This section should show the top 5 students ranked by number of completed enrollments,
display each student's name, company, number of completed courses, and average satisfaction
rating, use a card-based layout with a trophy icon, and place it above the enrollment table
on the dashboard.

Examine the plan — notice it identifies all required metadata: a new Apex method getTopLearners(), updates to the learningManagementDashboard LWC (HTML, JS, CSS), and the SOQL needed to aggregate enrollment data.

Iterate if you want

Plan mode makes no file changes. Tell Agentforce "Also show their department" or "Use a leaderboard table instead of cards" and it will revise the plan. Adjust until it matches your vision before proceeding.

9. Fast-Forward to Prebuilt Project 8 min

To keep everyone on the same baseline, we'll clone a curated repository that contains the same functionality Agentforce would scaffold.

16 Clone the repository

In Agentforce Vibes: Menu → File → Close Folder, then Menu → Terminal → New Terminal. Paste the following (click Allow if prompted):

cd ..
cd codebuilder/
git clone https://github.com/sadhananandakumaralmny/learning-management-vibes

17 Open the folder

Click the File icon → Open Folder → select learning-management-vibes → OK.

Explore the folder structure: the updated Apex controller with getTopLearners(), the modified LWC files, and test classes. Right-click any file to run Code Analyzer.

Finally, flip the switch from Plan to Act before the next step.

10. Live Preview 5 min

Before deploying, preview the component locally — no deployment round-trip required.

18 Start Live Preview

In the terminal, run:

sf lightning dev app

A browser tab opens automatically. Navigate to Connected Executive Education — the dashboard loads with your local component code, including the Top Learners widget. Edits to HTML or CSS update the browser instantly.

19 Try a quick tweak

While Live Preview is running, ask Agentforce:

Change the Top Learners section header color to dark blue and make the trophy icon larger.

Watch the browser update within seconds. When done, press Ctrl + C to stop the local server.

11. Deploy to Sandbox & Verify 10 min

20 Deploy the project

In the Agentforce conversation pane:

Deploy this project to my sandbox.

Confirm the target org when prompted. Agentforce uses MCP tools to handle the deployment, asking for confirmation at each stage.

21 Verify in the sandbox

Click the window icon from the bottom pane to open the org. Go to App Launcher → Connected Executive Education. The Top Learners widget should now appear above the enrollment table.

What you should see

A leaderboard showing the top 5 students with completed course counts, companies, and satisfaction ratings — with trophy icons and visual indicators.

12. Make a Small Change & Redeploy 13 min

Development is iterative. Let's add an Attendance Percentage column to the widget — demonstrating the full instruct → scaffold → deploy → verify loop.

22 Request the change

Add an average attendance percentage to each student in the Top Learners widget.
Show it as a progress ring or percentage badge next to their name.

Agentforce may ask for clarification — choose the option to proceed. Watch it dynamically fix errors as it goes through the Act loop.

23 Verify the update

Once deployed, hard refresh the sandbox (Cmd + Shift + R) to see the attendance percentage on each student card.

Agents are non-deterministic

You might not see the same response as the example above. The agent iteratively self-corrects until it reaches a clean deployment — this is the Act loop in practice.

13. Promote to Production with DX Inspector 5 min

The Top Learners widget is working in the sandbox. The promotion path is the same as Part 1 — just with more components.

24 Open DX Inspector and review the diff

Source: fullcopy sandbox  |  Target: production

ComponentTypeStatus
LearningManagementControllerApexClassModified — new getTopLearners() method
learningManagementDashboardLWCModified — updated HTML, JS, CSS

Click each component to review the diff. Confirm no unintended changes or debug code are included.

25 Deploy to production

  1. Select both components
  2. Click Deploy to Target
  3. Apex tests run automatically during validation
  4. Confirm and deploy
  5. Verify in production: App Launcher → Connected Executive Education — Top Learners widget should be live
Notice the pattern

Whether it's one picklist value or a full LWC feature, the promotion path is identical — sandbox, review, DX Inspector, production. The tooling scales with complexity; the workflow doesn't change.

Take-Home Prompts

Course Popularity Chart

Add a "Popular Courses" section to the learningManagementDashboard that shows the top 5
courses by enrollment count, with a horizontal bar chart showing how full each course is
(Current_Enrollment__c vs. Max_Enrollment__c). Include the instructor name and rating.

Revenue Summary

Add a revenue summary card to the dashboard showing total Amount_Paid__c across all
enrollments, broken down by Payment_Status__c (Paid, Pending, Scholarship, etc.)
as a donut chart with the total in the center.

Student Search Enhancement

Enhance the existing search bar on the dashboard to also support filtering by Country__c
and Role__c using dropdown filters alongside the text search.

Certification Tracker

Build a new "Certification Tracker" section showing students who have completed courses
where Certification_Offered__c is true, whether Certificate_Issued__c is true or false,
and the Certificate_Date__c. Highlight overdue certificates in red.
