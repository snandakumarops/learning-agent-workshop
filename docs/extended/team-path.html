Coordinated Development

🕓 5 minutes

You've seen what a solo admin can do. Now let's look at Coordinated development — how teams operate at scale, with source-tracked development, built-in change management, and release pipelines.

What's different at team scale?

The Solo path works beautifully for individual contributors. But when you have multiple admins, developers, and release managers working in parallel, you need more:

  • Source tracking — every change is versioned and attributed
  • Branching and isolation — teams work in parallel without stepping on each other
  • Automated pipelines — changes move through environments on a defined schedule
  • Release management — approvals, gates, and rollback built in

This is where DevOps Center comes in — Salesforce's native team development platform.
