Add the Deferred Status

🕓 10 minutes
⚠️ Stop — check you're in a sandbox

Before you make any changes, confirm the org you're logged into is your fullcopy sandbox, not production. Check the org name in the top-right user menu.

In the Solo path, you'll play the role of an admin making a quick, surgical change on your own — then promoting it to production safely. It's two steps: add a new picklist value in the UI, then review and deploy it with DX Inspector.

The business team has flagged a problem: students who need to pause their studies and rejoin a future cohort are being marked as Withdrawn — but Withdrawn implies they've left the program permanently. They need a separate status: Deferred.

This is a simple picklist change made directly in Setup. No code, no IDE.

See the gap

1 Open an Enrollment record

From the fullcopy sandbox, open the App Launcher and search for Connected Executive Education. Click into the Enrollments tab and open any record.

Click Edit and look at the Status dropdown. You'll see: Not Started, In Progress, On Hold, Withdrawn, Completed. No Deferred.

Why not just use "On Hold"?

On Hold is admin-controlled — typically used for a payment issue or admin hold. Deferred is student-initiated: they've chosen to move to a future cohort. The distinction matters for reporting, dashboards, and any automations downstream.

Add the picklist value in Setup

2 Open Object Manager

Click the gear icon → Setup. In Quick Find, type object manager and click Object Manager. In the list, search for and open Enrollment.

3 Open the Status field

Click Fields & Relationships in the left panel, then find and click the Status field.

4 Add the new value

Scroll to the Values section and click New. Enter Deferred as the value label and click Save.

The value is live in the sandbox immediately — no deployment needed for this step.

Verify

5 Check it on a record

Go back to any Enrollment record, click Edit, and confirm Deferred now appears in the Status dropdown.

Checkpoint

Deferred is selectable in the sandbox. The change is done — but it only exists here. Next we'll use DX Inspector to compare this sandbox against production and promote it safely.
